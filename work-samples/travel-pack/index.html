Project Overview

This travel pack is a comprehensive client deliverable designed for a high-end travel booking engagement. It demonstrates the level of detail, professionalism, and client-focused thinking that distinguishes executive virtual assistant work from basic travel booking services.

The package includes not just logistics (flights, hotel, activities) but also personalized support, day-by-day guidance, pre-departure preparation, and 24/7 availability—turning a travel booking into a seamless luxury experience.

📋 What's Included

  • Pre-departure checklist (documents, packing, prep)
  • Personal travel guide & support contact
  • Itinerary summary (date/time/event table)
  • Flight schedule with carrier details
  • Hotel confirmation with pricing breakdown (KSH)
  • Detailed day-by-day logistics
  • Activities & attractions with pricing
  • Travel tips & safety information
  • Emergency contacts & support availability
