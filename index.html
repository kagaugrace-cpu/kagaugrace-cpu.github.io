What I Can Help With

Practical support for the day-to-day work of running a business.

I offer three levels of involvement depending on what you need — from focused administrative help to broader operations support. All engagements are remote and available across time zones.

Level 01

Administrative & Calendar Support

Good for founders or managers who need consistent help managing their inbox, schedule, and daily correspondence — without the overhead of a full-time hire.

  • Email management and drafting responses
  • Calendar scheduling and coordination
  • Document preparation and formatting
  • Meeting notes and follow-up tracking

Best for: solo founders, small teams

Level 02

Operations & Process Support

Suited to businesses that need more than task execution — help setting up workflows, documenting processes, and keeping projects moving across teams.

  • Workflow documentation and SOPs
  • Project tracking setup (Asana, ClickUp, Trello)
  • Vendor and supplier coordination
  • Progress reporting and data tracking

Best for: growing teams, multi-project environments

Level 03

Ongoing Virtual Assistant Retainer

For clients who want a consistent, reliable VA on an ongoing basis — handling both administrative and operational tasks as a regular part of their team.

  • Everything in Levels 01 & 02
  • Dedicated hours each week or month
  • Flexible scope based on your priorities
  • Single point of contact for operations

Best for: busy founders who want consistent support

Not sure which level fits? Send a message and we can figure it out together.
